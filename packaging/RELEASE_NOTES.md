- **A simpler workflow.** One Overview screen walks through photos, number examples, analysis, the optional AI helper and tagging.

## Install

**Windows 10 / 11** — download `3EDIC-Setup-*.exe` and run it. No administrator rights needed. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click *More info → Run anyway* (the installer is not code-signed yet).

**macOS** — download the `.dmg` for your Mac (`arm64` = Apple silicon M1 and newer, `x86_64` = Intel), drag 3EDIČ to Applications. The first time, macOS may say it cannot verify the app (it is not notarized yet): open *System Settings → Privacy & Security* and click *Open Anyway*.

The first start downloads about 145 MB of models once. Your projects and models are kept when you update or uninstall.

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of every file.
